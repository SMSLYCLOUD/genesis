## About

**Genesis** is a book that builds a complete theory of intelligence, reality, and consciousness from a single primitive: the **distinction** — the irreducible act of separating *this* from *not-this*.

Starting from this one operation, the book derives:
- Why reality is different for different observers (R(S) = I(S))
- Why logic works (and why it almost didn't)
- How gravity creates the stage on which all other physics acts
- Why human-AI collaboration produces insights neither can reach alone
- A new branch of mathematics — **Distinction Dynamics**
- Practical applications from gravity-powered computing to anti-aging
- The first falsifiable experiment to test the entire framework

## The Core Equation

```
dD/dt = F(D, x, ∇D)
```

Where D is the distinction structure, x is the state within that structure, and ∇D is the gradient of new distinction formation. This is the equation of motion for the space of possibilities itself.

## The Seven Axioms

1. **Existence** — There exists at least one distinction D₀
2. **Dynamics** — Distinctions evolve: dD/dt = F(D, x, ∇D)
3. **Composition** — Distinctions compose: D₁ ⊗ D₂ → D₃
4. **Threshold** — Critical values D* exist where qualitative behavior changes
5. **Self-Reference** — A distinction can apply to itself: D(D) → fixed point
6. **Constraint** — Forbidden regions in D-space increase effective density
7. **Conservation** — Total distinction capacity of a closed system is constant
